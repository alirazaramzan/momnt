Photography in Pakistan runs on trust. We make it visible.

Today it runs on DMs, screenshots and word of mouth. Momnt puts verification, booking, gigs, learning and vendors in one calm place.

Brand promise

Every moment, in trusted hands.

Clients know who is real. Photographers get found, paid and delivered fast. Mentors teach. Vendors reach clients directly.

Brand purpose

To make Pakistan's photography community verifiable, bookable and teachable, and to keep every moment safe from first message to final gallery.

What we observed

Clients cannot tell who is genuine, because portfolios get copied. Prices and availability take endless messages to find.

What we also observed

Photographers lose hours sending 20 MB RAW files, then receive screenshots back. Good work still travels by word of mouth.

Positioning

For clients, photographers, mentors and vendors in Pakistan, Momnt is the verified platform where booking, gigs, learning and vendors meet, with clear prices and fast previews.

Framing note: we describe the opportunity we see. We never claim nobody else offers any of this.

Refined, precise, quietly confident.

Personality: refined, precise, warm, generous, never loud. People hand us their IDs, their photos and their wedding date, so every screen must look careful and competent at once.

A name that sounds like a blink.

Momnt drops a vowel and keeps the sound, like a shutter closing on a second. In text write Momnt with a capital M. In the logo it is lowercase with a gold full stop.

What we borrow, and what we leave

Editorial luxury

Borrow: large high-contrast serif, generous empty space, hairlines. Leave: crests, "Est." dates, heritage pastiche.

Film contact sheets

Borrow: crop marks, frame numbers, camera-style captions. Leave: sprocket holes, fake grain borders.

Precision software

Borrow: crisp tables, calm status chips, a true dark mode. Leave: gradient blobs, neon, glassy cards.

Translated for Pakistan

Photographs do the cultural work: mehndi marigolds, nikkah ivory, baraat gold, walima candlelight. The interface stays neutral so it never fights a client's colours. Original elements only. No reference logo, name or layout is copied.

Avoid: camera-icon clichés, stock smiles, heavy gold and glitter, gradient washes, bubbly rounded shapes, more than two decorative elements in one layout.

Usage

  • Clear space equals the height of the m on every side.
  • Minimum width: wordmark 72 px or 18 mm, seal 20 px or 6 mm.
  • Colours: midnight, chalk, brass on midnight only, or one flat ink.

Never

  • Stretch, tilt, outline, shadow or glow it.
  • Set the full stop in any colour other than brass or the surrounding ink.
  • Put the wordmark across a photographer's image. Momnt only watermarks its own content.

Production brief: redraw from Bodoni Moda, kern by eye, export SVG, PDF and transparent PNG in midnight, chalk and brass. Thicken hairlines under 64 px.

Colour rule for photographs

Brand colour lives in the frame, never in the photograph. No tints, overlays or filters on a photographer's work. Dark mode is the default for proofing and gallery screens. Light mode for marketing, search and dashboards.

Design tokens

Layout

12-column grid, 1120 px maximum, 24 px page margin. Spacing in steps of 8. Hairline borders. No drop shadows, except a single soft lift on menus and dialogs.

Shape

Cards, inputs and buttons use a 2 px radius. Chips and avatars are fully round. Primary button is solid midnight, secondary is outlined. Brass never fills a button.

Motion

One calm moment on load, then movement only in response to the person: 200 ms ease, fade and a small rise. Respect reduced motion.

Quality floor

Mobile first, visible keyboard focus, 4.5:1 text contrast, alt text on every portfolio image, empty states that tell the person what to do next.

Calm, exact, and a little bit generous.

How we write

  • Say what happened. "Booking accepted for 14 December" beats "Great news!"
  • Short sentences. Plain words. Sentence case everywhere.
  • Buttons name the action: Book this date, Send quote, Submit selection.
  • Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it. No apologies, no blame.
  • No exclamation marks in product copy.

We never write

  • "Capture your special moments"
  • "Stunning", "world-class", "revolutionary"
  • "Your big day"
  • "Seamless experience"
  • Anything that implies a photographer's work is ours
Sample microcopy. Empty gig board: "No gigs match yet. Widen your city or event type." Verification: "Under review. We check documents within 2 working days." [CONFIRM TIMING]

Quick reference

Logo

Lowercase momnt with a brass full stop. Focus mark as the seal. Clear space is one m. Never over a photographer's image.

Colour

Midnight #0A1428, chalk #F1F0EC, brass #C2A267, mist #E3E5E8, slate #5B6578, verified #1E7A5C, alert #B4423A. Brass is for lines only.

Type

Bodoni Moda for statements at 32 px and above. Manrope for everything functional. Sentence case.

Elements

Crop marks, camera caption, brass hairline, verified seal, gold dot, contact strip. Two per layout, at most.

Photography

True colour, no tints, always credited. Dark galleries, light dashboards.

The two tests

"This looks like it was made with care." and "I trust this with my photos and my date." If a screen passes only one, it is not finished.

Not yet covered: print stationery, full social post specs, email templates, Urdu typesetting.
